Sandbag Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts bags with division and rounding-up: the courses a height takes, the bags a run takes, the weight of the fill, and the cost per foot of run. The heights, the bag length, the fill weight and the prices are yours to measure or read off your own supplier ticket. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your site and does not know your water, your ground, your seams or your local flood guidance, and it gives no verdict that a wall of bags keeps water out or keeps anyone safe. For a real flood, follow your local authority. Every number here is your own entry.

The courses truth

Courses: the wall height divided by the course height, rounded up.

The bags truth

Bags: the run in inches divided by the bag length, rounded up, times the courses.

The weight truth

Weight: the bags times the pounds of fill in each, and the tons that makes.

The cost truth

Cost: the bags at their price plus delivery, and what that is per foot of run.
