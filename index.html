Sandbag Math

A wall 16 inches tall in courses of 4 inches takes 4 courses.

Boundary: arithmetic only Sandbag Math counts bags with division and rounding-up: the courses a height takes, the bags a run takes, the weight of the fill, and the cost per foot of run. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your site and does not know your water, your ground, your seams or your local flood guidance, and it gives no verdict that a wall of bags keeps water out or keeps anyone safe. For a real flood, follow your local authority. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 16 inch wall in 4 inch courses, a 20 ft run, bags 18 inches long, 40 lb of fill a bag, $1.25 a bag and $25 delivery.

The courses truth

A wall 16 inches tall in courses of 4 inches takes 4 courses.

The bags truth

The 20 ft run in bags 18 inches long takes 14 bags a course, 56 bags over 4 courses.

The weight and cost truths

The 56 bags at 40 lb hold 2240 lb, or 1.12 tons, and at $1.25 with $25 delivery they cost $95.00, or $4.75 a foot over the 20.
